## Overview

When Wantedly launched its Engagement Suite in January 2020, I joined the team in April to lead development. The suite comprised three distinct products targeting different dimensions of employee engagement.

## Products

- **Perk** — Employee benefits and perks management
- **Pulse** — Member condition and wellbeing monitoring
- **Story** — Internal communications and company newsletter platform

## Role

Served as team lead with primary ownership of the Story product, overseeing its design, development, and release. Coordinated across the three product tracks to ensure a coherent suite experience.
