## Overview

Joined Viibar (now VideoTouch) as the 8th employee directly out of university. Built a two-sided marketplace platform connecting video creators with clients who want video production.

## Responsibilities

- Backend development using Ruby on Rails
- Contributed to platform architecture and feature development across the full lifecycle
- Served as Scrum Master, facilitating agile ceremonies and continuous improvement for the engineering team

## Context

Working at an early-stage startup with a small team meant wearing many hats — shipping features, maintaining infrastructure, and helping the team work effectively. The Scrum Master role developed skills in facilitation, communication, and team dynamics alongside technical depth.
