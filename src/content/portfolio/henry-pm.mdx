## Overview

Joined Henry as a Product Manager, focusing on the planning and strategy for Henry's core product — an electronic medical record (EMR) and receipt computer system. The role involves deeply understanding the medical domain and translating clinical workflows into product decisions.

## Responsibilities

- Product planning and roadmap strategy for the EMR/receipt computer Henry
- Working with engineers, designers, and medical professionals to define requirements
- Bridging the gap between technical capabilities and healthcare domain needs

## Context

Henry aims to bring the first major innovation to the Japanese medical records space in 25 years. The challenge of entering a specialized, regulation-heavy domain without prior medical background became a driver for developing deep domain expertise through direct fieldwork and stakeholder engagement.
